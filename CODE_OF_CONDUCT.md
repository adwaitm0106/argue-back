# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to contribute, ask questions, and report problems, regardless of experience level, background, or how you found your way here.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good faith. Disagree about code, not about people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person who wrote it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/adwaitm0106/argue-back/issues) or, for sensitive reports, through a private message to a maintainer via their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and addressed as promptly as possible. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), simplified for a small open source project.
